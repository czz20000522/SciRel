# Synthetic ring-source handoff

The rows are ideal axisymmetric circular filaments in vacuum. Each `total_kA_turns` value is the signed total ampere-turns for one whole bank in one setting, not a current to apply to every ring. `bank_share` is a dimensionless fraction of that bank's total assigned to its row; the shares within each bank sum to one and are not equal. Ring and target coordinates are cylindrical [R,Z] in metres. These files do not describe a measured coil, finite conductor, plasma current, material boundary or uncertainty model.
